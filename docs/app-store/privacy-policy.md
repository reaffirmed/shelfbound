# Shelfbound privacy policy

_Last updated: November 25, 2026_

Shelfbound is a reader for books, comics and web fiction. It is built so that your reading stays on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. Shelfbound has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer receives no data about you or what you read.

## What stays on your device

- **Your library:** the works you save, your reading positions, highlights, notes, glossaries and settings.
- **Files you import:** EPUB, PDF and CBZ files are copied into the app's own storage so they can be read offline. Deleting a work in the app deletes its copy.
- **Repositories and sources you add:** the addresses you enter and the sources you install.
- **Sign-ins to other sites:** if you sign in to a site through a source, you do it on that site's own page. Shelfbound stores only the site's sign-in cookie, in the iOS Keychain. It never sees your password.

This data is included in your device's iCloud or computer backup, like any app's data, and in any library backup file you choose to export.

## Network connections

Shelfbound connects only to the repositories and sites you choose to add. Those sites see a normal web request from your device, including your IP address, and their own privacy policies apply. Shelfbound does not route your requests through any server of ours.

On iOS 18 and later, on-device translation uses Apple's Translation framework, which runs on your device.

## Notifications and background refresh

If you allow notifications, Shelfbound checks your saved works for new chapters in the background and tells you about them. These checks go directly from your device to the sites involved.

## Purchases

If Shelfbound offers a subscription, payment is handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details, only Apple's confirmation of what your Apple Account is entitled to.

## Children

Shelfbound is not directed at children under 13 and is rated for adults in the App Store.
