# App Store listing draft

Everything here is a starting point. App Store Connect enforces the limits noted.

## Name (30 characters)

Shelfbound: Books & Comics

## Subtitle (30 characters)

EPUB, PDF and CBZ, offline

## Promotional text (170 characters, can change any time)

Your books and comics on one shelf. Import EPUB, PDF and CBZ files, pick up where you left off, and read the way you like: paged, right to left, or one long strip.

## Description

Shelfbound puts your books, comics, manga and webtoons on one shelf.

IMPORT YOUR FILES
• EPUB books, PDF documents and CBZ comic archives
• From Files, iCloud Drive, or "Open in Shelfbound" from any app
• Chapters come from the book's own table of contents; comics keep their folders as chapters

READ YOUR WAY
• A text reader with themes, fonts, spacing and margins, saved per book
• Paged reading left to right or right to left, with panel-by-panel zoom
• A vertical reader for long-strip comics, with auto-scroll
• Highlights and notes you can export as Markdown
• On-device translation on iOS 18 and later

KEEP YOUR PLACE
• Continue reading from the Library or a Home Screen widget
• Organize by series, sort by recent or title
• Back up your library to a file

EXTEND IT
• Add a repository of extensions to read from other sources you choose. Shelfbound includes none and lists none.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no analytics, no ads. Everything stays on your device.

## Category

Primary: Books. Secondary: Entertainment.

## Age rating

Answer the questionnaire honestly. "Unrestricted web access" is yes, since sources can fetch arbitrary sites, and that alone puts the app at the top rating (18+ in Apple's current system, which replaced 17+). Everything else is "none" for the app itself.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Choose **Data Not Collected**. This matches `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` in the app.

## Support URL and marketing URL

Support URL is required. The repository's README works, or a GitHub Pages site. Marketing URL is optional.
